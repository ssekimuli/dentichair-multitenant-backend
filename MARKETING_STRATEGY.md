Ask these questions:
1. "How many staff do you have doing admin work today?"
2. "What's the biggest time drain right now?"
3. "Are you managing multiple locations or just one?"
4. "How's your insurance claim process working?"
5. "What percentage of invoices take more than 30 days to collect?"

### Presentation (5 minutes)
Focus on THEIR pain points from discovery, not generic features:

**If admin is the issue:**
"What we see is that when appointments auto-remind via SMS and invoices auto-follow up, 
you're looking at 70% less time on those tasks. Most practices free up 10+ billable hours/week."

**If collections is the issue:**
"When you have real-time payment status and automatic overdue reminders, you collect 30% 
faster. We had one practice go from 45-day average collections to 18 days in 90 days."

**If multi-location is the issue:**
"Instead of logging into 3 different systems, everything is in one dashboard. You see all 
locations simultaneously. Financial visibility across your entire practice in real-time."

### Close (3 minutes)
```
"Here's what makes sense: Let me send you a quick video showing exactly how it works 
with practices like yours. Takes 8 minutes to watch.

Then if it looks interesting, we can do a proper demo where you see YOUR data.

Sound good?"
```
