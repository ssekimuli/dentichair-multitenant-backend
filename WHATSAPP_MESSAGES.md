## 🎯 QUICK COPY-PASTE TOOLKIT

**If in doubt, use these:**

1. **Opener:** "How much time on admin each week? Most say 12-15 hrs. We cut that to 2-3 hrs. Chat?"

2. **Follow-up:** "Still interested in that demo? No pressure. Just want to show you the 10 hours/week you could save."

3. **Closer:** "Worth 15 min to see how practices like yours earn 30% more and work 70% less on admin?"

4. **Urgency:** "Only 5 spots left for free demo this week. In or out?"

5. **Specialist:** "Built for [Specialty]. Want to see how we track [specific pain point]?"

---

**Tips for WhatsApp Sales:**
- Keep it to 2-3 sentences max
- Use emoji sparingly (1-2 per message)
- Include clear CTA (demo, link, call time)
- Personalize with names when possible
- Send during business hours (9-5)
- Wait 24hrs between follow-ups
- Track which messages get responses
- Use A/B testing on templates

Good luck! 🚀
